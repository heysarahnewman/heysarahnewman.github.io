About

I’m a DrPH student in Implementation Science at the Johns Hopkins Bloomberg School of Public Health. I study how large organizations take on health-related policies and programs — especially the gap between how things are designed, how they’re implemented, and how they’re actually experienced.

My interests include qualitative methods, data analysis, and how AI might support thoughtful public health research. I also work full time outside of academia. This site is where I share selected work, current projects, and ongoing interests.
